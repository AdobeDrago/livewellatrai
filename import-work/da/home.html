Enrolling in Benefits

The annual Open Enrollment period for 2027 benefits has ended. However, life happens!

Experiencing a life change?

If you experience a qualifying life event (such as marriage, divorce, birth of a child, or loss of coverage due to a job change), you can update your benefit elections within 61 days after the event.

New hires

Welcome! We’re happy to have you here.

As a new hire, you have 61 days after your start date to make your benefit choices. If you don’t enroll by then, you will not be able to enroll in benefits until the next Open Enrollment unless you experience a change in family status.
